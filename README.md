# Gather Care concept (unofficial)

An unofficial design concept by Gaurav Basantani (https://gauravbasantani.com): one Gather Care screen where a clinician checks the summary of a patient's phone check-in. The call heard a new pill as "hydroxyzine"; her discharge list and pharmacy claim say hydralazine, and one tap corrects it. Unofficial concept, not affiliated with Gather Health. The patient, clinician and data are made up, and nothing here is medical advice. Live page: https://gauravbasantani.github.io/gather-health-concept/
